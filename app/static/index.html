DocChat

RAG · chat with a PDF, grounded in the text

Upload a PDF and ask questions. The app chunks and embeds the document, retrieves the passages most relevant to your question, and has Claude answer only from those passages — with citations. No hallucinated facts: every claim points back to a numbered source you can read.

chunk→ embed→ retrieve→ ground→ cite

—
Retrieval-only mode. No ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is set, so the app shows the passages it retrieved but can't generate the written answer. Set a key to turn on Claude's grounded answers.

Answer

Retrieved passages

How
Text is split into overlapping chunks, each embedded with a local sentence-transformers model. Your question is embedded the same way, and cosine similarity finds the closest passages. Those passages — and only those — are handed to Claude with instructions to answer from them and cite each claim. Retrieval is free and local; only the final answer calls the model.
Pipeline: sentence-transformers retrieval + Claude generation · grounded, with citations
